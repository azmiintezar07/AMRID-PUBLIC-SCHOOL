About AMRID Public School

Where Learning Meets Values & Safekeeping

AMRID PUBLIC SCHOOL, located at Fatma Nagar, Mandel Baisi in Purnia, Bihar, has been dedicated to delivering high-quality education since its foundation in 2020. Offering learning opportunities from Nursery to Class 8, our primary goal is to shape capable, disciplined, and morally strong citizens.

We understand that modern education requires a fine balance between core academic skills and human values. Therefore, alongside the standard educational curriculum, we place special emphasis on Islamic Basic Studies to provide student-focused moral grounding.
